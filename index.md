Painting FAQ

## House painting on Cape Cod, answered.

The questions Cape Cod homeowners ask us most about interior painting, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, and hiring a painting contractor.

### How much does it cost to hire house painters on Cape Cod?

The cost of a house painting project on Cape Cod depends on square footage, the condition of the surfaces, how much prep and repair is needed, and the paint you choose. A single room, a full interior repaint, a cedar shingle exterior, and a kitchen cabinet refinishing job all price very differently. Sartori Corporation gives every homeowner a free, no-obligation, written estimate after an on-site walkthrough, so you see the real number for your home before anything is scheduled. Call [(508) 246-3038](tel:5082463038) for your free painting quote.

### Do you offer free painting estimates on Cape Cod?

Yes. Every estimate is free and carries no obligation. We meet you at your home anywhere on Cape Cod, walk the project together, discuss colors, finishes, and timing, and send a detailed written proposal that itemizes prep, primer, paint, and labor. There are no hidden fees and no pressure to book.

### Which towns on Cape Cod does Sartori Corporation serve?

We are a Cape Cod painting company serving every town on the Cape, including Barnstable, Bourne, Brewster, Chatham, Dennis, Eastham, Falmouth, Harwich, Hyannis, Mashpee, Orleans, Osterville, Provincetown, Sandwich, Truro, Wellfleet, and Yarmouth, plus Boston's South Shore in Cohasset, Duxbury, Hingham, Marshfield, Plymouth, Scituate, Norwell, and Hanover. If you don't see your town listed, call us, chances are we cover it.

### Are you a licensed and insured painting contractor?

Yes. Sartori Corporation is a Massachusetts-registered Home Improvement Contractor (HIC #217650), fully insured, family-owned, BBB accredited, and painting homes on Cape Cod since 2013. See our [full credentials](https://sartoricorporationusa.com/licenses-insurance.html).

### What is the best time of year to paint a house exterior on Cape Cod?

On Cape Cod, exterior painting season generally runs from late spring through fall, when temperatures stay consistently above 50°F and humidity is manageable. That said, the best window depends on your specific home, its exposure to salt air, and the product being applied. We book exterior painting projects year-round and schedule each one for the conditions that will give the finish the longest life.

### How often should a Cape Cod home be repainted?

Coastal weather is hard on paint. Salt air, wind-driven rain, and intense summer sun all shorten the life of an exterior finish, and ocean-facing elevations, cedar shingles, and trim usually need attention before the rest of the house. Interiors last considerably longer. Proper surface prep and premium paint are what stretch the interval, which is why we never shortcut either. If you're unsure where your home stands, we'll tell you honestly during the free walkthrough.

### Do you paint or refinish kitchen cabinets?

Yes, cabinet painting and cabinet refinishing are among our most-requested services. We remove doors and hardware, degrease, sand, prime, and spray-finish for a factory-smooth, durable surface that holds up to daily kitchen use. Refinishing your existing cabinets costs a fraction of a full kitchen replacement. See our [cabinet refinishing](https://sartoricorporationusa.com/painting/gabinetes.html) page for details.

### What paint brands do you use?

We work exclusively with premium coatings: Benjamin Moore, Farrow & Ball, and Fine Paints of Europe. These paints cost more per gallon, but they deliver deeper color, better coverage, and longer life in Cape Cod's coastal climate. We'll recommend the right product line for your surface, exposure, and budget.

### Do I need to move out or move furniture while you paint?

No. We move and protect furniture, mask floors, trim, and fixtures, contain dust, and clean the work area at the end of every single day. Daily site cleanup is non-negotiable for us, when we leave, the only thing you should notice is the work.

### How long does a painting project take?

It depends on the size of the project, the condition of the surfaces, and how much prep is required, and for exterior work, on the weather. A single room is a very different job from a whole-home repaint or a full cabinet refinishing. The exact timeline is agreed with you when your estimate is finalized, and once it's set, we deliver on it, with weather-related exceptions flagged up front rather than discovered halfway through.

### Do you do interior and exterior painting, or only one?

Both. Sartori handles [interior painting](https://sartoricorporationusa.com/painting/interior.html) (walls, ceilings, trim, doors, staircases), [exterior painting](https://sartoricorporationusa.com/painting/exterior.html) (siding, cedar shingles, decks, porches, shutters), [cabinet refinishing](https://sartoricorporationusa.com/painting/gabinetes.html), and [doors and windows](https://sartoricorporationusa.com/painting/portas-janelas.html). Many clients bring us in for a whole-home project so one crew carries the same standard from the front door to the back deck.

### Do you handle surface prep, carpentry, and power washing?

Yes. Prep is the difference between paint that lasts and paint that peels in two seasons. Power washing, soft washing, and full surface preparation are part of our exterior process: we wash, scrape, sand, and prime before a single finish coat goes on. We use soft washing (a lower-pressure, chemical-assisted wash) on delicate surfaces like cedar shingles and roofing where high-pressure water could cause damage. We also handle carpentry, including replacing rotted wood and trim, so the finish goes onto a sound surface. On coastal homes we pay particular attention to sun-facing and salt-exposed elevations, where failure always starts.

### Can you help me choose paint colors?

Yes, and it's included at no additional cost. Color consultation is the first step of our process, and our color experts help you choose a palette that works with your home's natural light and architecture. Choosing a color from a two-inch chip is how people end up repainting twice, so we'd rather spend the time with you up front.

### Do you use subcontractors?

No. Sartori Corporation is family-owned, and the crew that started with Douglas in 2013 is the same crew that shows up at your home. You get consistent people, consistent standards, and one point of accountability, not a rotating cast of subcontractors who have never worked together.

### Do you offer a warranty on your painting work?

Yes. Your warranty is included with your written estimate, so you can read the terms before you commit to anything, no fine print and nothing to chase down later.
